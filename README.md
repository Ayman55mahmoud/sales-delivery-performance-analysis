# Sales & Delivery Performance Analysis

An Excel-based data analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance, customer segments, product preferences, and delivery operations through a business dashboard.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores sales and order data to provide a clearer view of business performance and delivery efficiency. The dashboard brings key metrics and visualizations together to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Understand order distribution across sales platforms and geographic areas.
- Identify best-selling products and product color preferences.
- Analyze customer segments: individuals versus companies.
- Evaluate on-time delivery performance.
- Review delivery status, including cancelled and undelivered orders.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- PivotTables
- Formulas and KPI Calculations
- Charts and Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design
- Business Data Analysis

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard covers the following metrics:

- **Total Sales:** Overall sales value.
- **Total Orders:** Number of orders recorded.
- **Average Selling Price:** Average selling price based on the selected calculation method.
- **Sales by Platform:** Comparison of sales performance across platforms.
- **Best Seller:** Identification of the best-selling product.
- **On-time Delivery Rate:** Percentage of eligible orders delivered on time.
- **Delivery Status Breakdown:** Distribution of delivery outcomes, including cancelled and undelivered orders.
- **Orders by Area:** Geographic distribution of orders.
- **Best-Selling Colors:** Comparison of product colors by sales frequency.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Individual versus company customer distribution.

## 🔍 Analysis Process

1. Reviewed the source data and available fields.
2. Cleaned and prepared the data for analysis.
3. Calculated sales and order performance metrics.
4. Analyzed sales across platforms, products, and geographic areas.
5. Evaluated delivery performance and order statuses.
6. Built charts and organized the results into an Excel dashboard.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help businesses identify sales patterns, understand customer and product demand, monitor delivery performance, and identify areas that may require further investigation.
